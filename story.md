# The Something in the Forest

## Overview

The National Park of **(REDACTED)** has been placed under emergency lockdown following a sudden wave of unexplained disappearances. No consistent patterns have been identified. Survivor testimonies do not match. MDP units have been placed on standby at the outer perimeter.

You have been assigned to a remote ranger post. Your duty is simple: help those who escape the forest, and identify who is responsible.

---

## The Characters

### Mike — The Hiker

Mike was hiking alone through the forest when the trees began to move toward him. In the chaos, he spotted a tall figure — **Roy** — running in the same direction. The two stuck together, fleeing through the darkness until the ranger cabin finally came into view.

During their escape, Roy confided in Mike: he had lost a friend named **Ara** back at the river bend. Roy looked terrified. Whatever he saw out there, Mike didn't want to see it too.

Mike is cooperative but scared. He knows the creature tracks voices and will share what he learned from Roy if pressed.

### Roy — The Lost Friend

Roy lost his friend **Ara** at the river bend. One moment she was behind him, the next — gone. Only the scratching in the trees remained.

Before they split, Ara had told Roy something critical: **the creature mimics people**. Roy saw something in the dark that moved like Ara, but it didn't walk right. The wrongness of its gait still haunts him.

Roy and Ara split at the river — she went east, he went west. He considers it the biggest mistake of his life.

### Ara — The Observer

Ara knows how to spot the creature. She watched it for **three nights from the ridge**, studying its patterns. She discovered that the creature follows a **three-night cycle** along the ridge — and that it watches you back when you look away.

Ara and **Zen** had planned to meet at the ranger post, but Zen warned her the place was compromised. Ara decided to chance it anyway. She figured Zen was just paranoid.

Ara's intel is real and valuable. She is the most knowledgeable survivor about the creature's behavior.

### Zen — The Paranoid

Zen told Ara the ranger post was a trap. She was right about everything — she just didn't realize how right.

Zen doesn't trust **Isabel**, who runs the radio out of the north cabin. Zen believes Isabel knew they were coming before they did. Way before. According to Zen, Isabel has been feeding false reports into the radio, and the chain of information she keeps mentioning is a loop that leads back to her.

Zen tried to warn Ara away from the post. Ara didn't listen.

### Isabel — The Keeper of Records

Isabel runs the radio out of the north cabin. She expected the new ranger three days ago. She keeps meticulous records of every visitor, every testimonial, and every death.

Everyone who passes through the forest ends up mentioning Isabel's name last. She knows it. She finds it funny.

The truth is darker: **Isabel is the killer**. She maintains her records not to help, but to control the narrative. She knows exactly who the creature has taken and when. The "unknown man" she mentions at the end of her chain doesn't exist — it's a fabrication to cover her tracks.

### The Lost Hiker

A desperate soul who just wants to go home. They noticed someone living in the north cabin — someone who never comes out during the day. That someone was Isabel.

### The Park Scout

Part of the official search party. The scout lost contact with the south patrol six hours before reaching the post. They found **tracks near the river** — two sets, one human, one not. The inhuman tracks **disappeared mid-stride**, as if the creature simply stopped touching the ground.

### The Wanderer

They've been in the forest for days. Before reaching the post, they met **Mike** — alive, scared, but determined. The wanderer thought the cabin was a dream until they saw the light in the window.

## The Timeline of Events

### Day 1: The Beginning

The lockdown begins. Mike arrives at the ranger post, fleeing from the forest with tales of moving trees and a tall figure. Radio broadcasts warn all units to avoid the north perimeter. A survivor is found three kilometers south of the post in critical condition.

### Day 2: The River

Roy arrives, frantic about losing Ara. The radio reports that the south patrol has gone silent — their last transmission: *"It walks like us. It talks like us. Don't let it in."* The creature has been observed mimicking human voices, repeating the names of those it has already taken.

### Day 3: The Ridge

Ara and Zen arrive together. Ara shares her three nights of observation: the creature no longer avoids the cabin — it is approaching. Three more disappearances occur overnight. The chain of sightings spans the entire park. The creature does not cast a shadow under direct moonlight.

### Day 4: The Truth Approaches

Isabel arrives. MDP requests permission to breach the perimeter. The survivor count inside the cabin reaches its highest point. The creature stands perfectly still outside for hours at a time, observing. It attempted to mimic a survivor's voice to gain entry — the mimicry was nearly perfect.

### Day 5: The End

The perimeter collapses. The final radio broadcast warns: *"Whatever you do, do not let it in."* All sightings now originate from directly outside the cabin. The creature is waiting.

## The Creature

The entity in the forest is humanoid but wrong. Its skin is grey and mottled. Its nails are darker than human, possibly elongated. It does not cast a shadow under moonlight. It can stand motionless for hours, observing.

The creature mimics human voices with terrifying accuracy, repeating the names of those it has taken. Survivors report a guttural clicking sound preceding each sighting — a pattern that repeats every 14 seconds.

It follows a three-night cycle along the ridge. It is intelligent, choosing its targets deliberately. It knows the layout of the park.

Most disturbing: its tracks disappear mid-stride, as if it can simply stop touching the ground whenever it chooses.

---

## The Mystery

The disappearances have no consistent pattern because there are **two predators** in the forest:

1. **The creature** — an inhuman entity that mimics, hunts, and takes people in the dark.
2. **Isabel** — a human who uses her position at the radio to manipulate, misdirect, and eliminate survivors.

Isabel keeps her records to maintain control. She falsifies radio reports. She knows who is coming before they arrive. And she ensures that every chain of information eventually leads back to her.

The ranger's job is not just to survive — it is to see through the deception, protect the innocent, and identify the true killer among them.

Those who escape the forest are not safe until Isabel is exposed.

## Themes

- **Paranoia vs. intuition** — Zen's paranoia is justified. Dismissing gut feelings in this forest is fatal.
- **Mimicry and deception** — The creature mimics voices. Isabel mimics authority. Both hide in plain sight.
- **Trust** — Every survivor has information. Every survivor has bias. The ranger must decide who is reliable and who is compromised.
- **Isolation** — The remote post, the collapsing perimeter, the silence of the south patrol. Help is not coming.
- **Records as weapons** — Isabel's records seem helpful but are tools of control. Truth is only powerful when it's in the right hands.
